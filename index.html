THE STORY BEHIND THE PROJECT

From a Spreadsheet to a Warband HQ.

Warband HQ started a few years ago with a simple Google Sheets spreadsheet. As a World of Warcraft player, I wanted a convenient way to track the progression of my characters. I built a spreadsheet connected to the Blizzard API using OAuth 2.0, allowing me to retrieve and organize character information.

Over time, I started sharing my work with friends from the game, as well as my brother and his wife, who also play World of Warcraft with me. Seeing that other players enjoyed using it made me realize that this little personal project could become something more.

So I started thinking: why not turn it into a website? A web application would allow me to go beyond what a spreadsheet could offer, introducing new features and making character progression easier to manage and organize.

And that's how Warband HQ was born: a personal project that grew from a simple spreadsheet into a dedicated companion for World of Warcraft players.
